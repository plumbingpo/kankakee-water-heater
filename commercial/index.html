Food Service Hot Water

Hot Water for Kitchens, Restaurants, and Food Facilities

Dish machines, sanitizing sinks, and handwashing stations all compete for the same hot water, and a heater that's undersized or failing shows up first as a dish machine that can't hit temperature and stacks of dirty plates. A high-temp dish machine typically needs final rinse water near 180°F, which almost always calls for a booster heater paired with the main system. We handle high-input tanks, tankless racks, booster heaters, and peak-demand sizing, and we schedule every visit around your prep and service times.

Hot water temperature is a critical issue for any food-service operator. Health inspectors check water temperature at handwashing sinks and sanitizing stations, and a dish machine that can't reach its rinse temperature puts the whole operation at risk. Our work keeps your system delivering the temperatures and volume your operation depends on, with written service records that demonstrate a well-maintained kitchen to inspectors and landlords. You get detailed reports after each visit, along with the records many leases and inspectors ask for.

We work around your business, not the other way around. We coordinate every replacement and maintenance visit with owners, managers, and kitchen staff. Off-hours installation is available when needed, and downtime stays short so the kitchen opens on time.

Safety

Installation, Inspection, and Record-Keeping

Commercial property owners have a duty of care to provide safe conditions for employees, customers, and tenants. A poorly installed or neglected water heater can lead to carbon monoxide from a blocked or undersized vent, scalding at the tap, bacterial growth in tanks kept too cool, and costly water damage from a failed tank. Kankakee Water Heater provides commercial installations and inspections that cover venting and combustion air, gas piping sizing, relief valve and discharge line routing, expansion tanks, thermostatic mixing valves, drain pans, seismic strapping where required, and permits and inspections. Our written reports identify risks, assign priority levels, and recommend specific preventive actions.

Regular professional service protects the people and property you're responsible for, and following through on our recommendations helps keep minor issues from becoming liabilities. It also creates a record. We maintain service logs, temperature records, and a documentation binder for every account, and we increase visit frequency for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, laundries, and mechanical rooms. When a landlord, insurer, or inspector asks for proof of due diligence, it's ready.

Rapid Response

Fast Commercial Water Heater Service

A hot water failure on a commercial property can shut down a kitchen, cancel a day of salon appointments, leave hotel guests with cold showers, and expose the owner to tenant disputes. Kankakee Water Heater provides fast emergency water heater service for commercial properties throughout Kankakee and surrounding areas, and we schedule non-urgent work after hours or before opening to limit downtime.

Failed gas valves, burned-out elements, ignition faults, tripped high-limit switches, leaking tanks, stalled recirculation pumps, tankless lockouts: we see all of it on tank, tankless, and boiler-fed systems. Our first priority is diagnosing the fault and getting hot water back as quickly as possible, often by isolating the failed heater so the others carry the load. Once it's running, we put maintenance in place to prevent a repeat, and each response is fully documented for your facility files.
